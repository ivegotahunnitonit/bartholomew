What is Bartholomew?

The Sub-Millisecond AI Trajectory Observability & Security Layer for Autonomous Agents. See inside any stack, any app, at any scale, anywhere with real-time OWASP 2026 kill-switches and SHA-256 cryptographic attestations.

1. The Problem: AI Bots Can Make Mistakes

Companies today use AI programs to talk to customers, look up passwords, generate code, and make decisions automatically. But sometimes, these AI bots can get tricked, accidentally leak private company passwords, or get stuck doing the same action over and over in an infinite loop.

Analogy: Imagine hiring a new assistant who is incredibly fast, but occasionally leaves the key to your bank safe on a public sticky note.

2. The Solution: Bartholomew is the Security Guard

Bartholomew acts as a sub-millisecond spell-checker and security guard for AI robots. Every time an AI bot tries to perform an action, Bartholomew inspects it in 0.000001 seconds to ensure it is safe before allowing it to proceed.

Analogy: Bartholomew is like a high-tech airport X-ray scanner that checks every piece of luggage before the plane takes off.

3. Why Bartholomew Stands Out

Older monitoring tools (like Datadog or Sentry) were built years ago for regular web pages, not for smart AI agents. Bartholomew is built from scratch in Golang and Python to specifically monitor AI reasoning steps and enforce global OWASP security standards.

Bottom Line: Bartholomew makes AI software faster, safer, and 100% compliant with enterprise regulations.
