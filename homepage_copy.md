## Subheadline

## Intro
Bartholomew is the authorization layer for autonomous agents. It sits between the agent and the real-world action and decides whether the action should be allowed before it happens. By enforcing policy at the action boundary, Bartholomew helps teams stop unsafe commands, destructive workflows, credential leaks, and prompt-injection-driven misuse.

## Key Points
- Policy-aware allow / deny decisions
- Proof of action through receipts and attestation
- Telemetry for autonomous agent observability
- Governance for tool use and execution risk
- Built for developers shipping agentic workflows

## Short Product Sentence
Bartholomew is the gate between autonomous agents and real-world actions.
